about me

i like the idea of giving people their time back. i'm fascinated by what humans can achieve when they're not bogged down by repetitive tasks. that's why agentifying everything is so exciting.

i grew up in hospitals, and worked as a hospital pharmacy tech compounding sterile iv meds.

then i spent seven years in healthcare sales, across software, pharma and ai-enabled medical devices. i was always the one training the team on the new tech. now i build the agents.

building them has shown me the difference between what ai can do and what it only pretends to do, and how much human judgment goes unnoticed until you try to reproduce it.
