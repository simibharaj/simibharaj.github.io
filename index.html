04 · how i work

the human in the loop.

i spent seven years inside healthcare: the hospital floor, the pharmacy and the sales side. i was always the one training the team on the new tech. now i build the agents.

building them has shown me the difference between what ai can do and what it only pretends to do, and how much human judgment goes unnoticed until you try to reproduce it.

if you've never done the job, your agent is just adding to the slop.
